What Lichen Controls

Three systems. One platform.

Instead of separate boxes for environment, irrigation and lighting that don't share data, Lichen brings all three under one roof.

01 · Environment

Keep every zone in its optimal range.

Automatic vent control that reacts to real conditions, not a clock.

  • Temperature & humidity monitoring — live readings from every zone, charted alongside VPD.
  • Automatic vent control — Basic, Advanced Temp, or Smart Climate modes that factor in VPD and humidity together.
  • Fan control — independent ramp-up and full-speed thresholds for supplementary air movement.
  • Manual override, always — take direct command of any actuator, then hand it back.
02 · Irrigation & Fertigation

Water on what the soil needs, not a fixed clock.

Trigger straight from live sensor readings across every bed.

  • Real-time soil moisture watering — dosing triggered by live readings, not just a schedule.
  • EC & pH tracking — feed strength and acidity monitored continuously per zone.
  • Water level sensors — tank and reservoir levels visible alongside every other reading.
  • Scheduled watering windows — up to five daily windows per actuator, set once and forgotten.
03 · Lighting

Top up daylight without wasting it.

DLI tracking keeps supplemental lighting working with the sun, not against it.

  • PAR & DLI tracking — daily light integral calculated automatically and charted alongside your readings.
  • Lighting schedules — up to five daily windows per actuator, set once and forgotten.
  • Zoomable history — see exactly when supplemental light kicked in across the last 30 days.
  • Manual override — take direct control of any light bank, then hand it back.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Does this replace my existing controllers, or work alongside them?

Lichen is designed to control the vents, pumps and lighting hardware you already have — it adds sensing, automation and remote access on top of your existing structures rather than requiring a rebuild.

How many zones or sites can it manage?

Multi-farm management is built in — run unlimited growing spaces from a single account, each with its own sensors, crops and control settings.

What sensors are available?

Air temperature and humidity, soil moisture, temperature, EC and pH, water level, and PAR — added to the network as your growing space grows.

Does automation keep running if the internet drops?

Yes. The hub runs on your local network and keeps executing your automation rules locally — you're not dependent on an external cloud service to keep your crop safe.

How is device security handled?

Every device is issued its own unique, revocable credentials rather than sharing a single password, and node uptime is tracked so a dropped connection is visible immediately.

Can I export my data or move it elsewhere?

Yes — one-click backup and restore covers your entire farm setup, and your data lives on your own hardware from day one.
