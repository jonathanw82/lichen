The Story
You didn't start growing to babysit a thermometer.

You started because there is something quietly extraordinary about coaxing life from a seed. The first green shoot breaking through dark soil. The smell of a greenhouse in full growth. The satisfaction of a harvest you raised with your own hands.

But greenhouses are unforgiving. A warm afternoon that tips into a scorching afternoon. A vent left closed on a humid night. A crop that looked fine on Tuesday and was lost by Thursday. Every grower knows that feeling. The one that arrives too late.

"What if your greenhouse could think for itself — so you never had to worry about it again?"

That question is exactly why Lichen exists.

What Is Lichen
The intelligent system that never sleeps.

Lichen is a complete greenhouse intelligence platform. It lives on your network, connects wirelessly to sensor nodes placed throughout your growing spaces, and monitors everything — temperature, humidity, ventilation, light levels, soil moisture — around the clock, in real time.

And it doesn't just watch. It acts.

When temperatures climb too high, Lichen opens your vents — automatically, precisely, without you needing to be there. When conditions return to the right range, it closes them again. It adapts to your greenhouse, learns its rhythms, and keeps your plants in the environment they need to thrive.

All of it visible, at a glance, on any phone, tablet, or computer — and installable straight to your home screen. Wherever you are.

What Lichen Does
Everything your greenhouse needs. Nothing you don't.

Built for a single hobby greenhouse or several growing spaces at once — every feature scales the same way.

📊

Farms & Dashboards

  • Multi-farm management — run unlimited growing spaces from one account, each with its own sensors, crops and controls.
  • Three-tab dashboard — Overview gauges, historical Graphs, and a dedicated Control tab for every farm.
  • Live sensor gauges — temperature, humidity, CO₂, soil moisture, EC, pH, water level and PAR, updated in real time.
  • VPD & DLI tracking — vapour-pressure deficit and daily light integral calculated automatically and charted alongside your readings.
  • Zoomable history — range and granularity controls from the last hour out to 30 days.
⚙️

Smart Automation

  • Automatic vent control — Basic (single target temperature), Advanced Temp, or Smart Climate modes that add VPD and humidity into the decision.
  • Lighting & watering schedules — up to five daily windows per actuator, set once and forgotten.
  • Real-time soil moisture watering — trigger watering straight from live soil sensor readings, not just the clock.
  • Fan control — independent ramp-up and full-speed thresholds for supplementary air movement.
  • Manual override, always — take direct command of any actuator in a tap, then hand control back to Lichen.
📡

Sensors & Hardware

  • Plug-and-play auto-discovery — new sensors and leaf-nodes appear automatically the moment they're connected, no pairing wizard.
  • Expandable sensor network — add air temperature/humidity, soil moisture/temperature/EC/pH, and water-level sensors as your growing space grows.
  • Per-device security — every device gets its own unique, revocable credentials, never a shared password.
  • Node health & uptime — live online/offline status with a full uptime history per leaf-node.
🌱

Crops & Seed Vault

  • Seed Vault — a personal seed library: variety, family, sowing window, germination time, cost per pack, notes, CSV import/export.
  • Full crop lifecycle — sow, germinate, plant out and harvest, with flows tailored to fruiting, leafy greens, herbs and root veg.
  • Harvest & yield records — log single or cumulative multi-pick yields and see your most productive varieties.
  • Seed cost tracking — cost-per-seed is snapshotted at sow time, so later price changes never rewrite history.
  • Sow-by-month planner — see what's ready to sow this month, drawn straight from your Seed Vault.
🔒

Reliability & Access

  • Installable app — add Lichen to your phone's home screen; the app shell still opens when you're offline.
  • Local weather integration — hourly and five-day forecasts alongside your live greenhouse readings.
  • One-click backup & restore — export and restore your entire farm setup.
  • Runs on your network — no cloud fees, no subscriptions. Your data stays on your own hardware.
