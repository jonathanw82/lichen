The Problem

You can see what's happening. You can't act on it fast enough.

A commercial site rarely fails because nobody was watching. It fails because the person who could act was in another tunnel, in a meeting, or asleep — and by the time anyone checked the readings, the window to fix it had already closed.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Does Lichen work with the glasshouses and polytunnels I already have?

Yes. Lichen is designed to sit alongside your existing structures and actuators — it adds sensing and automation to the vents, irrigation and lighting hardware you already run, rather than requiring a rebuild.

Do I need a cloud subscription to run it?

No. Lichen is offered on a yearly subscription, but that's for the platform itself — not a cloud hosting fee. Once it's set up, it runs on your own network and hardware, and your sensor data stays on-site under your control. Contact our sales team for pricing.

Can it handle more than one site or growing space?

Yes — multi-farm management is built in from the start. Run unlimited growing spaces from a single account, each with its own sensors, crops and control settings.

How long does setup take?

Sensors and leaf-nodes are plug-and-play — they're auto-discovered the moment they're connected, with no pairing wizard. Most single-zone setups are live the same day.

What happens if the network goes down?

The installable app shell still opens when you're offline, and automation continues running locally on the hub — you're never dependent on an external cloud service to keep your crop safe.

Can my team keep manual control when they need it?

Always. Every actuator can be manually overridden in a tap, and handed back to automatic control just as easily.
